# AFTERMATH Roadmap

## v0.0.3 — Weapon Death States and Executions

The following features are intentionally deferred from v0.0.2:

- Weapon-specific full corpse disassembly and death-state selection for blunt, edged, shotgun and other future attack classes.
- A downed-enemy state and close-range ground execution system.
- Short modular execution animations such as repeated strikes and wall executions.

These require melee weapons, shotgun mechanics, downed-state rules and execution input that do not belong in the v0.0.2 Nightclub combat-feel scope.
